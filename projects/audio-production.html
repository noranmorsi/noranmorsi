Rep

Rep is a bold, wide-ranging podcast, brought to you by journalist Noor Tagouri. Noor began by examining the misrepresentation of Muslims in US media and how this impacts American culture. She discovered a fuller exploration of the ever-evolving story of America, which challenged the stories we’ve known and revealed how the stories we tell affect all of us.

Produced by Noran.

From Cairo to Brooklyn: The Arab American Experience

This is a story of the experience of Arab Americans in the U.S. I interview Rehab Morsi, and her son Hussein Mohamed, about their experience for the past 20-something years in the U.S. I also interview Iraqi-American playwright Heather Raffo, who contextualizes the Arab-American experience from her point of view.

Mesh Zanbek: The Report

Theatre / Writing / Directing • 2021

“The Report” is a play I wrote as part of Mesh Zanbek, a theatre series about sexual harassment in Egypt. I also directed the theatre performance. The work was later published by AUC Press in It’s Not Your Fault: Five New Plays on Sexual Harassment in Egypt.

About

Noran Morsi is a New York-based visual journalist and producer, and brings a diverse blend of creative skills to storytelling.

From Cairo, Egypt, she is passionate about journalism, theater, and TV, with a love for illustration.

Noran is currently an Editorial Coordinator at Bloomberg Live Experiences, where she brings captivating global events to life, from San Francisco to Johannesburg, New York to Los Angeles.

Opinions are my own.
